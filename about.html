Bio​

I am Guang-Ze Yang, a Robotics & Control R&D Engineer with a background in mechanical systems engineering and control theory. My public research focuses on robust and safety-critical multi-agent formation control. I am extending this foundation toward robot learning, manipulation, and physical intelligence.

I publish code and technical notes as youkoutaku. This portfolio brings together my personal public research and engineering work.
